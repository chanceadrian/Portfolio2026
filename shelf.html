Shelf

The objects of your life.
Shelved beautifully.

Shelf displays your favorite things in a way that is more thoughtful. More beautiful. And ultimately more personal. It's a remarkably simple display case for photos, notes, links, and more — one built on a profound belief: that the way we organize our lives shapes how we experience them.

  • Your photos.
  • Your notes.
  • Your music.
  • Your links.
  • And voice.

Shelf lets you hold on to things worth revisiting in a bold new way.

Spaces & Rooms

Space for every side of you.

Create dedicated spaces for work, travel, study, relationships, hobbies, and the things you return to most.

Style

Shelf‑Expressive.

Shelf comes in every shade you do. Create spaces that feel personal, lived-in, and unmistakably yours — where the things you keep shape the atmosphere around them.

Huge in almost every way. Your collection takes advantage of available space, so you can reflect on your life from anywhere.

Reflection

All shelves hold things. This one makes the most of them.

  • Right on time. Shelf can surface meaningful items throughout your day, based on time, place, routines, and the moments most connected to them.

  • Shelf reflection. Revisit anniversaries, favorite objects, and the moments around them, all reimagined for the biggest screen in your home.

  • Widget posters. Place meaningful objects directly into your space with visionOS, turning memories, reflections, and favorite items into part of your environment.

  • Keep something close. Pin Shelf items, deliveries, or memories to widgets across your devices for lightweight reflection throughout the day.

Shelf Mail

Share your favorite things with the ones you love.

  • Shelf Mail. Receive curated items, rooms, and spaces from the people who know you best, all in one dedicated mail room inside Shelf.

  • Share. Send a single item, a full room, or an entire space through Shelf or Messages, curated and delivered like a personal gift.

  • Attach entries. Attach journal entries to anything you share, so memories, reflections, and relationships travel with the objects that hold them.

  • Schedule. Schedule items, rooms, or entire spaces to arrive later, for birthdays, anniversaries, or the moments that deserve a little anticipation.

Behind the Design.

  • Walk into someone's home. The things people choose to display reveal something important: what they love, what they remember, and who they're becoming.

  • Understanding how people organize their lives. The project was shaped through ongoing conversations, prototype reviews, and observational research exploring how people collect, display, revisit, and emotionally relate to meaningful artifacts.

  • Designed to feel lived in. Color, atmosphere, and spatial organization were treated as part of the experience itself — helping collections feel expressive, personal, and inhabited instead of visually neutral.

  • Designed around thoughtful display. Shelf explores how selection, arrangement, and revisitation can help digital collections feel more personal and meaningful.

  • New emotional contexts. Shelf draws from the intuitiveness of physical shelving systems while exploring how digital spaces can better support memory, identity, and reflection.
